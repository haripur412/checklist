Instructions

  • Fill in all applicable parameters for each equipment and tap Save Inspection Form before moving to the next item.
  • Saved entries stay on this device until you tap Confirm & Finalize Submission — make sure you have a working internet connection at that point.
  • In text fields, avoid starting an entry with =, +, -, or @, as these may be misread as formulas after submission.
  • Your name can only be set once and cannot be changed afterward, so enter it carefully at first use.
